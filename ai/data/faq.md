"""SafariDesk operator FAQ used by the keyword-first concierge."""

### What time do game drives start?
Game drives start at 6:30am and 4pm. Please be ready ten minutes early at the lodge gate.

### Can I pay the balance on arrival?
Yes. The deposit is paid before departure by M-Pesa; the balance is paid in cash or M-Pesa at the end of the trip.

### What should I pack for the Mara?
Bring a warm layer for mornings, sun hat, sunscreen, binoculars and neutral-coloured clothing.

### Is there network coverage in the park?
Coverage is patchy inside the park. Your driver carries a satellite phone for emergencies.

### Do I need a visa to visit Kenya?
Most nationalities need an eTA before travel. Apply on the official Kenya eTA website.

### Can children join the safari?
Children are welcome. Under-fives ride free, and family vehicles can be arranged on request.

### What if it rains?
Trips run in light rain; the vehicle has a canopy. In heavy weather your guide adjusts the route for safety.

### How long is the drive from Nairobi to the Mara?
About five to six hours depending on road conditions, with a stop at the Great Rift Valley viewpoint.

### Are tips expected?
Tips are appreciated but never required. Most guests tip 5 to 10 percent for a great trip.

### Can you cater for vegetarian guests?
Yes. Tell us dietary needs when booking and the lodge prepares accordingly.
